# Client Discovery Questionnaire

Use this questionnaire before beginning design or development. The goal is to understand the business, the audience, the website’s purpose, and the practical details needed to plan, build, launch, and support the site.

---

## 1. Basic Business Information

1. What is the business name?
2. Is there a preferred spelling, capitalization, or abbreviation for the business name?
3. What service or product do you offer?
4. How would you describe the business in one or two sentences?
5. When did the business start?
6. Is this a new business, an existing business, or a rebrand of an older business?
7. What city or region is the business based in?
8. What areas do you serve?
9. Do customers come to you, do you go to them, or is the service fully online?

---

## 2. Website Goals

1. What is the main purpose of this website?
2. What action should visitors take after visiting the site?
3. Should visitors call, text, fill out a form, email, book an appointment, visit a location, or buy something?
4. What would make this website successful for you?
5. Are there any specific problems you want the website to solve?
6. Is this website mainly for credibility, lead generation, information, sales, bookings, or something else?
7. Are there any features you know you want right away?
8. Are there features you might want later, but not for the first version?

---

## 3. Ideal Customer

1. Who is the ideal customer?
2. What kind of person or business is most likely to need your service or product?
3. What problems, needs, or goals does your ideal customer have?
4. What questions do customers usually ask before hiring you or buying from you?
5. What concerns or hesitations might customers have?
6. What should visitors understand quickly when they land on the website?
7. Are you trying to reach local customers, online customers, or both?

---

## 4. Services, Products, and Offers

1. What are your main services or products?
2. Can you list each service or product with a short description?
3. Are there different packages, tiers, or price ranges?
4. Do you want pricing shown on the website?
5. Are there services or products you do not want to emphasize?
6. What is your most important or most profitable service?
7. Are there seasonal services, specials, or promotions?
8. Do customers need to request a quote or consultation?

---

## 5. Business Differentiation

1. What makes your business different?
2. Why should someone choose you over another business?
3. Do you have special training, experience, certifications, awards, or credentials?
4. Do you have a personal story behind the business?
5. What values should the website communicate?
6. What words should people associate with your business? For example: professional, friendly, affordable, luxury, creative, reliable, local, fast, family-owned, etc.

---

## 6. Branding and Visual Style

1. Do you already have a logo?
2. Do you have brand colors?
3. Do you have preferred fonts or a style guide?
4. Do you have photos, videos, illustrations, or other visual assets?
5. Do you need help choosing colors, fonts, or the overall look?
6. What kind of visual style do you want?
7. Are there any styles you definitely do not want?
8. Should the site feel more modern, classic, playful, elegant, bold, calm, artistic, corporate, or something else?
9. Do you have competitors or websites you like?
10. What do you like about those websites?
11. Are there competitor websites you dislike?
12. What do you dislike about them?

---

## 7. Content Needed

1. Do you already have written text for the website?
2. Do you need help writing or editing the website copy?
3. Do you have an About section or owner bio?
4. Do you have testimonials or reviews?
5. Do you have frequently asked questions?
6. Do you have photos of the work, products, location, team, or owner?
7. Do any photos need to be taken before launch?
8. Do you have social media profiles that should be linked?
9. Do you have business hours?
10. Do you have a physical address that should appear on the site?
11. Should the site show a service area instead of a physical address?

---

## 8. Pages and Site Structure

1. Do you want a one-page website or a multi-page website?
2. Which sections or pages do you need?

Possible sections/pages:

- Home
- About
- Services
- Products
- Gallery
- Testimonials
- FAQ
- Contact
- Booking
- Location
- Blog or updates

3. Which section is most important?
4. Is there any information that must appear near the top of the page?
5. Is there any information that should be in the footer?

---

## 9. Contact and Lead Capture

1. What is the best way for customers to contact you?
2. Should the website include a phone number?
3. Should visitors be able to text you directly?
4. Should the website include an email address?
5. Should the website include a contact form?
6. What information should the contact form collect?

Possible contact form fields:

- Name
- Email
- Phone number
- Service needed
- Preferred contact method
- Message
- Requested appointment date
- Location or service area

7. Who should receive contact form submissions?
8. Do you want an auto-reply message sent to customers?
9. Do you use a booking tool, calendar tool, CRM, or scheduling app?
10. Do you want the website connected to that tool?

---

## 10. Domain, Hosting, and Business Email

1. Do you already own a domain?
2. If yes, where was the domain purchased?
3. If no, what domain names are you considering?
4. Do you already have website hosting?
5. Do you need help setting up hosting?
6. Do you need email like `info@businessname.com`?
7. Do you already use Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or another business email provider?
8. Who should own the domain and hosting accounts?
9. Who should have access to make website updates after launch?

---

## 11. SEO and Local Search

1. What search terms would you like people to use to find your business?
2. What city, neighborhood, or service area should the website target?
3. Do you already have a Google Business Profile?
4. Do you need help setting up or updating your Google Business Profile?
5. Are there important keywords, services, or locations that should appear on the site?
6. Do you want customer reviews displayed on the website?
7. Do you have existing reviews on Google, Yelp, Facebook, or another platform?

---

## 12. Legal, Privacy, and Policies

1. Does the website need a privacy policy?
2. Does the website need terms and conditions?
3. Does the website need a refund, cancellation, or booking policy?
4. Are there disclaimers required for your industry?
5. Are there licenses, permits, or certifications that should be shown?
6. Are there any accessibility, industry, or legal requirements we should know about?

---

## 13. Timeline and Budget

1. When would you like the website completed?
2. Is there a specific launch date or event?
3. Are there parts of the website that must be ready first?
4. What is the budget for the project?
5. Are you expecting a simple first version now and improvements later?
6. How quickly can you provide content, photos, and feedback?
7. Who will make final decisions and approve the site?

---

## 14. Maintenance and Support

1. After launch, who will update the website?
2. How often do you expect changes to be needed?
3. What kinds of updates might be needed?

Examples:

- Text updates
- Photo updates
- New services
- Price changes
- Seasonal promotions
- Hours changes
- New testimonials
- New pages

4. Do you want ongoing maintenance support?
5. Do you want monthly check-ins or updates only when requested?
6. Do you want analytics or traffic reports?
7. Who should be contacted if something breaks?

---

## 15. Final Notes

1. Is there anything else I should know before planning the website?
2. Are there any concerns you have about the project?
3. Are there any examples of websites, designs, or businesses that capture the feeling you want?
4. What would make you excited to share this website with customers?
